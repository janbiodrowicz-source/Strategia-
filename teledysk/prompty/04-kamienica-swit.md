# 04: Kamienica o świcie (ujęcie 1, 0:00–0:07,5)

**Wynik:** ⏳ do wygenerowania.
**Technika:** jedno ujęcie bez postaci, szeroko 84°, kamera nisko na podwórku patrzy w górę, powolny najazd na jedyne zapalone okno. Generujemy 10 s, w montażu tniemy do 7,5 s (4 takty).
**Ciągłość:** fasady są w tym samym bladym ochrowym tynku, który widać za oknem kawalerki (`referencje/kawalerka.jpg`), a okno ma takie same białe drewniane ramy.

```text
SCENE CONTEXT
Dawn in the enclosed inner courtyard of an old Polish tenement. The whole building is still asleep except for one warm lit window on the fourth floor. Quiet, intimate, the city just before waking.

LOCATION MAP
A narrow square courtyard well, about 12 by 12 meters, closed on all four sides by five-storey tenement walls in pale ochre plaster, weathered and stained at the bottom. Rows of tall old double-casement windows with white wooden frames, small iron-railed balconies on some floors. Worn grey cobblestones on the ground, a single bare young tree in a small square of earth near the center, an old bicycle leaning against the left wall. Above, a small square of pale dawn sky framed by the roof edges. The camera stands on the cobblestones near one corner of the courtyard.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
The first visible frame already shows the courtyard walls rising around the camera and the one lit window, visible from the very first frame. No black frame, no fade-in.
The lit window is on the fourth floor of the far wall, on the right third of the frame, upper half. Every other window is dark, reflecting the cold pale sky.
Exactly one window glows warm; it stays the only lit window for the whole shot.

FORMAT MODE
Single continuous take. Real-time motion. No cuts.

OPTICS
84° diagonal field of view, wide-angle lens character, camera 14 meters from the far wall. The walls converge upward with natural wide-angle perspective; the cobblestones in the foreground are sharp, the whole courtyard in deep focus. The shot stays wide for its whole length; the push-in never becomes a close-up.

CAMERA
Camera at a low angle, 1.2 meters above the cobblestones, tilted up about 25 degrees toward the far wall. A very slow steady push-in on a dolly, about 1.5 meters across the whole shot, gently drifting toward the lit window. Smooth, no shake. Rule of thirds: the lit window holds the right third, the square of sky sits in the top of the frame.

ACTION TIMING
0:00 to 0:04: Still courtyard in blue dawn light. The warm window glows on the fourth floor. Two pigeons sit on a balcony railing on the left wall.
0:04 to 0:07: The pigeons take off and fly up out of the courtyard toward the square of sky. The first pale pink light touches the top edge of the roofs.
0:07 to 0:10: The push-in continues slowly; the warm window grows a little larger in the frame. A thin white curtain inside it moves gently.

PHYSICS
Pigeons fly with real wing beats and weight, rising in a curved path. The thin curtain moves softly in a slight draft. Faint breath of morning mist hangs low over the cobblestones and drifts slowly. Nothing else moves.

LIGHTING
Blue hour turning into dawn. The courtyard is filled with cool soft blue ambient light from the sky above, no direct sun on the walls. The single window glows warm tungsten amber, the only warm source in the frame, casting a soft warm spill on the window frame and sill. The roof edges catch a first thin line of pale pink light from 0:04. Palette: 60% cool dawn blue and grey, 30% pale ochre plaster in shadow, 10% warm amber from the window.

AUDIO
Quiet dawn city tone, distant tram passing far away, pigeons cooing, then the flutter of their wings. No music, no voices, no subtitles.

POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS
The courtyard is empty of people; only the two pigeons move. One warm lit window only. Real tenement architecture, natural textures of plaster and stone, real fine film grain, stable picture, no flickering.
```

**Na co patrzeć:**
- Czy zapalone okno jest **jedno** i widać je od pierwszej klatki. Jeśli model zapali więcej okien, wzmocnij linijkę „Every other window is dark”.
- Czy najazd nie robi się za szybki. W tym utworze intro jest spokojne.
- Dodatek do rozważenia w następnej iteracji (jedna zmiana naraz): o 0:07 za firanką przesuwa się sylwetka. To zapowiedź Ani, która w ujęciu 2 stoi już w kuchni.
