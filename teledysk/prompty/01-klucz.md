# 01: Klucz (kuchnia)

**Wynik:** ✅ działa (wygenerowane w innym narzędziu niż Higgsfield). Twarze zgodne z referencjami, ładne światło z okna. Model ustawił kamerę bardziej z przodu (3/4) niż z profilu, ale wyszło lepiej.
**Technika:** jedno ujęcie, 47° (normalny obiektyw), miękkie światło z okna, przerwana czynność (łyżeczka).
**Uwaga:** kwestia była po angielsku. Zgodnie z zasadą używamy wersji polskiej: `"Jest twój. Jeśli chcesz."` i dopisujemy `Voice @ANIA` z `postacie.md`.

SCENE CONTEXT
On a quiet morning in a small sunlit kitchen, a young woman has just pushed a single apartment key across the table to the man sitting opposite her. She is asking him to move in without asking directly.

ACTIVE REFERENCES

@TOMEK: early 30s lean man, tousled dark brown hair, short stubble, dark teal overshirt open over a white t-shirt. Relaxed morning state, teaspoon in his right hand. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Small bright apartment kitchen. A round light-oak table, 80 cm across, in the midground. A large window in the background center, morning sun coming through it, green plants on the sill. Two white ceramic mugs on the table. Camera stands at the side of the table, perpendicular to the line between the two characters.

FIRST FRAME AND SPATIAL BLOCKING
The first visible frame already contains both characters seated at the table in profile. No empty establishing frame.
@ANIA sits screen-left, torso facing screen-right toward @TOMEK. Her right hand rests on the table center, fingertips touching a single brass key lying on the wood directly in front of @TOMEK's mug.
@TOMEK sits screen-right, torso facing screen-left toward @ANIA, 1 meter across the table from her. His right hand holds a teaspoon inside his mug, mid-stir.
The key lies on the table between them, in the exact center of frame.

FORMAT MODE
Single continuous take. Real-time motion. No cuts.

OPTICS
47° diagonal field of view, standard normal lens character, camera 2.5 to 3 meters from the table, natural human-eye perspective. Natural face proportions, both faces sharp, the window and plants softly out of focus. No extreme wide distortion, no telephoto compression.

CAMERA
Camera at seated eye level on a tripod, very slow push-in of about 30 cm across the whole shot. Medium two-shot from the waist up: @ANIA holds the left third, @TOMEK holds the right third, the key and both hands readable on the table. Rule of thirds.

0:00 to 0:02 — @TOMEK stirs his coffee, the spoon clinking softly against the mug. @ANIA's fingertips rest on the key; her eyes flick between his face and the key.

The smile starts in the eyes, then the mouth — surprised and already certain.

Hair moves slightly with every head movement. Hands press naturally into the wood; the key stays one small brass key, the same size in every frame.

Faces lit softly by warm bounce from the light oak table and white walls, gentle and slightly low-key. Window highlights bloom softly. No flat front light, no studio key.
